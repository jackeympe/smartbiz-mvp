About SmartBiz Fire

Professional fire safety.
Delivered digitally.

SmartBiz Fire helps businesses stay safer, organised and prepared through professional fire-safety services and digital compliance management.

Our Mission

Fire safety is not just equipment.
It is an ongoing responsibility.

SmartBiz Fire was built around a simple belief: fire safety compliance should be manageable, visible and ongoing — not a folder of paper that gets neglected between inspections.

We work with restaurants, offices, warehouses, schools, factories and retail businesses across South Africa to deliver professional fire safety services backed by a digital operating system that tracks equipment, inspections and compliance dates.

Our philosophy: Inspect. Service. Comply. Protect.
