About SmartBiz Fire

Professional fire safety.
Delivered digitally.

SmartBiz Fire helps businesses stay safer, organised and prepared through professional fire-safety services and digital compliance management.

Our Mission

Fire safety is not just equipment.
It is an ongoing responsibility.

SmartBiz Fire was built around a simple belief: fire safety compliance should be manageable, visible and ongoing — not a folder of paper that gets neglected between inspections.

We work with restaurants, offices, warehouses, schools, factories and retail businesses across South Africa to deliver professional fire safety services backed by a digital operating system that tracks every piece of equipment, every inspection and every compliance date.

Our philosophy: Inspect. Service. Comply. Protect.

How We Operate

Technology-enabled. People-delivered.

SmartBiz Fire combines professional field technicians with a digital operations platform. Inspections, job assignments, equipment tracking and compliance documents are all managed through one system — giving every client a consistent, documented service experience.

Our internal workflow ensures that no inspection goes unrecorded, no certificate expires unnoticed, and no renewal is missed.
