About Our Organisation

Building South Africa's Fire Compliance Platform

SmartBiz Fire Safety was founded to eliminate the friction, paperwork, and missed renewals that put South African commercial properties and lives at risk.

Our Mission

To make fire safety and statutory compliance seamless, transparent, and audit-ready for property managers, business owners, and facilities teams across South Africa.

Digital-First Architecture

We combine hands-on accredited technician field service with a modern cloud platform featuring QR asset tagging, mobile photo evidence capture, and instant digital COCs.
