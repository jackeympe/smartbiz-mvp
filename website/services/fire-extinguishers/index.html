Fire Extinguisher Supply, Inspection & Servicing

Fire extinguishers are your first line of defense. South African occupational health & safety regulations mandate annual inspections and periodic pressure testing.

Dry Chemical Powder (DCP)

Class A, B, and C fires. Ideal for offices, commercial warehouses, vehicles, and industrial facilities. Available in 1.5kg, 2.5kg, 4.5kg, and 9kg capacities.

Carbon Dioxide (CO2)

Class B and electrical fires. Safe for server rooms, electrical distribution boards, and sensitive electronics without leaving residue. 2kg and 5kg units.

Foam & Water Extinguishers

Class A organic materials and Class B flammable liquids. Clean and effective cooling suppression for packaging and manufacturing sites.

Wet Chemical Extinguishers

Class F cooking oil and grease fires. Essential for commercial restaurant kitchens, hotel catering facilities, and bakeries.

What's Included in Every Annual Service:

  • Weight and pressure verification against statutory factory tolerances
  • Inspection of safety seals, discharge nozzles, hoses, and locking pins
  • Corrosion, denting, and mechanical damage checks on the cylinder body
  • Application of tamper-evident service tags and unique QR code asset labels
  • Immediate digital logbook update and Certificate of Compliance generation
