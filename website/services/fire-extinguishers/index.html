What does fire extinguisher servicing include?

A professional service should establish what equipment is present, inspect its condition, identify defects and determine what maintenance or corrective work is required. The exact work depends on the extinguisher type, condition, applicable requirements and manufacturer information.

Inspection

Check visible condition, accessibility, identification and obvious damage or defects.

Maintenance

Address serviceable maintenance requirements according to the equipment and applicable procedures.

Recharge or refill

Restore extinguishing media where required and appropriate to the equipment and service process.

Replacement advice

Identify equipment that is damaged, unsuitable or otherwise requires replacement.

Asset identification

Maintain clear equipment information so service history can be tracked.

Service records

Document completed work, defects and recommendations for a traceable maintenance record.

How often should fire extinguishers be serviced?

There is no single interval that applies to every extinguisher and every environment. The appropriate programme depends on the equipment, applicable standards, manufacturer guidance, environment and fire risk.

For commercial customers, the practical objective is a documented programme combining routine visual checks with scheduled professional servicing and corrective action.

What is the difference between inspection, servicing and replacement?

ActivityPurposeOutcome
InspectionIdentify visible condition, access and obvious defects.Equipment is recorded as satisfactory or an issue is identified.
ServicingPerform scheduled professional service and required maintenance.Service completed, defects recorded and documentation updated.
Repair or rechargeRestore equipment where the service process permits it.Equipment returned to service or escalated for replacement.
ReplacementRemove equipment that is unsuitable, damaged or otherwise not appropriate to retain.Suitable replacement equipment is specified and installed where required.

What types of fire extinguishers may businesses use?

Extinguisher selection should be based on the fire hazards present. Common categories include dry chemical powder, carbon dioxide, water and foam extinguishers. Wet chemical equipment can be relevant to particular cooking-fire risks.

Electrical equipment, kitchens, flammable liquids and ordinary combustible materials can present different fire risks. Equipment should therefore be selected for the hazard rather than simply choosing the cheapest or most common extinguisher.

How should a business choose a fire extinguisher servicing company?

Choose a provider based on verifiable technical capability, applicable registrations or credentials, relevant standards, service documentation and the provider's ability to manage corrective actions—not price alone.

  • Ask what is included in the service.
  • Confirm which equipment the provider can service.
  • Ask what documentation will be supplied.
  • Ask how defects and replacements are handled.
  • Request evidence of relevant technical credentials where applicable.
  • For multiple branches, ask whether equipment registers and recurring schedules can be managed.
