Fire Hose Reel Servicing in Gauteng

Fire hose reel servicing checks the visible condition, accessibility and service requirements of hose reel equipment. SmartBiz Fire provides hose reel inspections, maintenance recommendations and service documentation for commercial properties, schools, offices, retail sites and other workplaces.

What Does Hose Reel Servicing Check?

  • Hose condition and visible damage
  • Reel and mounting condition
  • Nozzle and fittings
  • Accessibility and visibility
  • Leaks and apparent defects
  • Water supply and testing where included in the agreed scope
  • Service records and corrective actions

Inspection

Identify visible defects and service requirements.

Maintenance

Address applicable maintenance requirements within the agreed scope.

Documentation

Record service findings and recommended corrective actions.

Follow-up

Quote for additional repair, replacement or related fire-protection work where required.

Who Needs Hose Reel Servicing?

Commercial buildings, schools, warehouses, offices, retail premises, restaurants and other properties with installed hose reels should maintain them as part of an appropriate fire protection programme.
