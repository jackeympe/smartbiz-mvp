Core Services

SmartBiz Fire provides end-to-end fire safety services — from equipment supply and servicing to compliance management and certificate workflows.

Fire Extinguishers

We supply, install, service and maintain fire extinguishers for commercial, industrial and public premises. Annual service inspections are conducted in line with SANS 10105 requirements. Extinguisher types covered include dry powder, CO₂, foam, water and wet chemical units. Each service is recorded digitally with the due date for the next inspection tracked automatically.

Hose Reels

Hose reel systems must be inspected and maintained to ensure they are operational in an emergency. We conduct pressure testing, inspect hose-reel cabinets, nozzles, valves and pipework, and provide written inspection records. Installation services are also available for new premises or replacements.

Fire Detection & Alarm Systems

We inspect and support fire detection systems including smoke detectors, heat detectors, manual call points and control panels. Inspections check sensor function, wiring integrity, alarm audibility and panel status. Findings are documented and recommendations are provided for any remedial action required.

🚨

Emergency Systems

Emergency lighting and exit signage are critical life-safety systems. We inspect emergency lighting units, test battery backup duration, check exit sign illumination and verify that signage placement meets requirements. Results are recorded with next-service dates tracked.

Compliance & Reports

Digital inspection records, service reports and Certificate of Compliance (COC) workflows are managed through the SmartBiz Fire operating system. Compliance documents are generated and delivered digitally. Renewal dates are tracked and reminders are issued before expiry — removing the risk of lapsed compliance.
