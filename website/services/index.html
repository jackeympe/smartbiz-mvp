How our fire protection service process works

  1. Scope: confirm your property, business type, location and required service.
  2. Inspect: review the relevant equipment, areas and conditions.
  3. Document: record findings, service work and outstanding actions.
  4. Correct: quote or complete agreed maintenance, replacement or installation work.
  5. Maintain: track future service requirements and renewal dates.

Fire protection services in Gauteng

SmartBiz Fire serves Gauteng subject to confirmed service coverage and project requirements. Businesses in Johannesburg, Pretoria, Tembisa, Midrand, Ekurhuleni and surrounding areas can request a quotation or inspection.

For multi-branch organisations, ask about organising service records and corrective actions by property.

How much do fire protection services cost?

Pricing depends on property size, location, equipment quantity and type, condition, testing requirements, parts, installation and corrective work. Request a quotation with the property location and known equipment requirements for a more accurate scope.
