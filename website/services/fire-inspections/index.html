Fire Safety Inspection Service

Fire Safety Inspections in South Africa & Gauteng

A professional fire safety inspection checks the condition, accessibility and apparent readiness of fire protection equipment and identifies hazards or corrective actions at your property. SmartBiz Fire provides structured inspections for businesses and commercial premises, with findings documented for follow-up.

What Does a Fire Safety Inspection Check?

A fire safety inspection reviews the property and its fire protection arrangements against the scope agreed for the inspection. It can identify visible defects, missing equipment, access problems, hazards and documentation gaps.

  • Fire extinguisher presence, condition and accessibility
  • Hose reels and related fire equipment
  • Emergency exits and evacuation routes
  • Fire safety signage and access
  • Visible electrical and combustible-material hazards
  • Equipment records and service information
  • Outstanding corrective actions

Fire Equipment

Check visible condition, location, accessibility and service information for applicable fire equipment.

Escape Routes

Review emergency exits, access routes, exit signage and visible obstructions within the inspection scope.

Fire Hazards

Identify observable ignition sources, combustible storage and other conditions requiring corrective action.

Digital Records

Record findings and recommended actions so the customer has a clear service and follow-up trail.

Fire Safety Inspection vs Fire Extinguisher Servicing

InspectionExtinguisher servicing
Reviews the property and identified fire-safety conditionsFocuses on servicing and maintaining fire extinguishers
Can identify wider hazards and corrective actionsAddresses the condition and service requirements of the equipment

A business may require both. The appropriate scope depends on the property, equipment and service requirement.

What Happens During a SmartBiz Fire Inspection?

  1. Site information: confirm the property, business type and inspection scope.
  2. Walkthrough: inspect relevant areas and fire protection equipment.
  3. Findings: document visible defects, hazards and missing requirements within scope.
  4. Recommendations: identify corrective actions, servicing or replacement requirements.
  5. Follow-up: provide the applicable service record or report and quotation for additional work where required.

Who Needs a Fire Safety Inspection?

Inspections can be relevant to businesses and organisations that need to understand the condition of their fire protection arrangements.

Offices

Review fire equipment, exits, signage and common workplace hazards.

Restaurants

Consider cooking areas, combustible materials, equipment and evacuation arrangements.

Warehouses

Review storage arrangements, access to fire equipment and visible fire hazards.

Schools

Consider occupied areas, exits, fire equipment and evacuation arrangements.

Retail

Review customer areas, stock, equipment access and emergency routes.

Factories

Consider machinery, ignition sources, materials and site-specific fire protection arrangements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fire safety inspection?

It is a structured review of a property's fire-safety arrangements within an agreed scope. It can identify visible hazards, equipment issues, access problems and corrective actions.

How often should a business have a fire safety inspection?

Frequency depends on the property, risk profile, equipment, applicable requirements and the organisation's maintenance programme. SmartBiz Fire can recommend an appropriate service scope after assessing the site.

Is a fire safety inspection the same as extinguisher servicing?

No. Extinguisher servicing focuses on fire extinguishers, while a broader inspection can review the property and multiple fire-safety conditions. Both services may be required.

Do you provide inspections in Gauteng?

SmartBiz Fire serves Gauteng subject to location and project requirements. Contact the team to confirm availability for your site.

Will I receive a report?

The documentation supplied depends on the inspection scope and work performed. SmartBiz Fire can provide applicable inspection findings, service records and recommendations.

Can you inspect multiple branches?

Yes. Multi-site inspections can be organised by property, allowing businesses to track findings and outstanding actions across branches.
