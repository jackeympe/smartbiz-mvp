Fire Hydrant Service

Fire Hydrant Servicing in Gauteng

Fire hydrant servicing reviews the condition and maintenance requirements of hydrant equipment within the agreed service scope. SmartBiz Fire supports businesses and property managers with inspections, testing where applicable, defect identification and documented corrective actions.

What Does Fire Hydrant Servicing Include?

The scope depends on the installation and required service. It can include:

  • Visual condition inspection
  • Hydrant accessibility
  • Valves, connections and visible fittings
  • Defect identification
  • Flow or pressure testing where included and appropriate
  • Maintenance recommendations
  • Service documentation
