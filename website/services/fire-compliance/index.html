Fire Compliance Services in South Africa

SmartBiz Fire helps businesses organise fire safety inspections, equipment servicing, service records, corrective actions and Certificate of Compliance workflows. The objective is a traceable fire-safety record that is easier for business owners, property managers and auditors to manage.

What is fire compliance?

Fire compliance is not simply having fire extinguishers on a wall. It involves maintaining appropriate fire protection arrangements, identifying defects, completing required maintenance and retaining evidence of inspections and service work for the property.

Requirements vary according to the building, occupancy, equipment, applicable standards and authority requirements. SmartBiz Fire therefore uses an inspection-led approach rather than treating every property as identical.

What SmartBiz Fire checks

A fire compliance inspection can review the condition, accessibility and service status of fire protection equipment and identify outstanding corrective actions.

Fire Extinguishers

Equipment condition, identification, accessibility, service status and maintenance requirements.

Hose Reels

Visible condition, accessibility, installation issues and applicable service or testing requirements.

Fire Hydrants

Condition, accessibility, connections and testing or maintenance requirements where applicable.

Fire Safety Records

Inspection findings, equipment registers, service history, defects and corrective actions.

Certificate of Compliance workflow

Where a Certificate of Compliance is applicable to the work and property, the SmartBiz Fire workflow is designed around inspection, documentation, sign-off and verification.

  1. Book a fire safety inspection.
  2. Inspect the property's fire protection equipment and identified requirements.
  3. Record findings, defects and corrective actions.
  4. Complete required service or corrective work where authorised.
  5. Generate the applicable compliance documentation after successful completion.
  6. Maintain the certificate and service record for future reference and renewal planning.

How much does fire compliance cost?

There is no single fire compliance price. Cost depends on property size, number and type of fire protection assets, inspection requirements, equipment condition, corrective work, documentation and location.

For an accurate quotation, provide the property location, business type, approximate equipment count and the service or compliance documents currently available.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is fire compliance?

It is the process of maintaining appropriate fire protection arrangements, equipment, inspection records and required documentation for a property and its use.

What documents can SmartBiz Fire provide?

Depending on the work, records can include inspection findings, service records, equipment registers, compliance documentation and applicable certificate outputs.

How often should fire equipment be serviced?

It depends on the equipment, standards, manufacturer requirements, environment and risk. Annual servicing is commonly used for portable fire equipment.

Can a SmartBiz Fire certificate be verified online?

Yes. The MVP includes an online certificate verification workflow for issued certificates.
