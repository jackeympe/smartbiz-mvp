Fire Risk Assessment

Fire Risk Assessment in Gauteng & South Africa

A fire risk assessment identifies fire hazards, evaluates existing controls and records practical corrective actions for a property. SmartBiz Fire provides structured assessments for businesses, schools, offices, retail premises, restaurants, warehouses and other workplaces.

What Does a Fire Risk Assessment Cover?

The assessment considers the property's activities, people, hazards and existing fire controls within the agreed scope.

  • Sources of ignition
  • Combustible materials and storage
  • Electrical and equipment hazards
  • Fire extinguishers and other fire equipment
  • Emergency exits and evacuation arrangements
  • Fire signage and access
  • Staff and occupant considerations
  • Existing controls and corrective actions

Identify hazards

Record observable conditions that could contribute to fire or make evacuation more difficult.

Evaluate controls

Review existing equipment, procedures and arrangements within the assessment scope.

Prioritise actions

Separate urgent defects from planned improvements so management can act.

Document findings

Create a practical record that can support internal fire-safety management and follow-up.

Fire Risk Assessment Process

  1. Confirm site and assessment scope.
  2. Walk through relevant areas and identify hazards.
  3. Review fire protection measures and evacuation arrangements.
  4. Document findings and recommended actions.
  5. Provide follow-up or quotation for identified services where required.

Who Needs a Fire Risk Assessment?

Businesses with staff, customers, stock, equipment, combustible materials or specialised hazards can benefit from a documented risk assessment. The scope should reflect the actual use and risk profile of the premises.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fire risk assessment?

It is a structured assessment of fire hazards, existing controls and corrective actions for a defined property or workplace.

Is a fire risk assessment the same as a fire inspection?

No. An inspection may focus on observable equipment and property conditions, while a risk assessment evaluates hazards and controls more broadly within its agreed scope.

Can you assess multiple branches?

Yes. Multi-site assessments can be organised by property so management can track findings and actions across branches.

Do you provide fire risk assessments in Gauteng?

Yes, subject to confirmed service coverage and project requirements.
