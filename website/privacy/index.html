Privacy Policy (POPIA Compliance)

Last updated: September 2026 · SmartBiz Fire Safety (Pty) Ltd

SmartBiz Fire Safety (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the personal information and privacy of our customers, clients, and website visitors in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act No. 4 of 2013 of South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

We collect company names, representative contact details (names, email addresses, phone/WhatsApp numbers), property site addresses, and fire safety equipment serial numbers solely for scheduling inspections, issuing statutory Certificates of Compliance (COC), and managing service renewals.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used strictly to fulfill statutory fire compliance inspections, generate audit-ready documentation, provide automated renewal notifications, and facilitate customer communications.

3. Data Protection & Security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell, rent, or trade customer information to third parties. Public QR codes and certificate verification links display only non-confidential equipment operational statuses.

4. Contact Our Information Officer

For POPIA inquiries or data access requests, please contact our Information Officer at compliance@smartbizfire.co.za.
