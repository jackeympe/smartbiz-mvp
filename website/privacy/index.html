Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026.

1. Introduction

SmartBiz Fire respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your data when you visit our website or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, business details, location and the details of the fire safety services you request. When you submit an inspection or quotation form, the information is transmitted to our backend system for processing.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process your inquiry, provide fire safety services, manage appointments, send service updates and comply documentation, and to contact you regarding your requirements. We may also use aggregate data to improve our services.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. Data is stored in secure systems and access is restricted to authorised personnel.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Certain functions of our operations are handled by third-party service providers, including email delivery, SMS notifications and database hosting. These providers are permitted to access your information only for the purposes specified by us and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us through the details provided below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact SmartBiz Fire via WhatsApp on +27 67 768 4582 or through our Contact page.
