Privacy Policy

How SmartBiz Fire collects, uses and protects personal information.

Effective date: 26 September 2026
Company: SmartBiz Fire (Pty) Ltd
Registration: 2025/515463/07
Country: South Africa

1. Introduction

SmartBiz Fire (Pty) Ltd respects the privacy of our customers, website visitors, suppliers, business partners and other individuals who interact with our services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal information when you use our website, request fire-safety services, book an inspection, communicate with us through WhatsApp or other channels, or otherwise interact with SmartBiz Fire.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable South African data-protection requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with SmartBiz Fire, we may collect:

  • Name and surname
  • Business or company name
  • Email address
  • Telephone and WhatsApp numbers
  • Business or service-site address
  • Service requirements and enquiries
  • Inspection and appointment information
  • Fire-safety equipment and servicing information
  • Quotes, invoices and transaction information
  • Compliance and certification information
  • Communications with SmartBiz Fire
  • Information submitted through our website forms
  • Technical information reasonably required for website security and operation

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to provide, administer, secure and improve our services.

3. How We Use Personal Information

SmartBiz Fire may process personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests
  • Schedule fire-safety inspections and appointments
  • Provide quotations and proposals
  • Deliver fire-equipment servicing and maintenance
  • Manage customers and service locations
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Send relevant service and compliance notifications
  • Manage recurring inspections and renewal reminders
  • Generate service records and compliance documentation
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our services and internal operations
  • Protect our systems against misuse, fraud and security threats
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and compliance obligations

4. Appointment and Google Calendar Information

When you request or book an inspection or other appointment, SmartBiz Fire may process your contact information, business name, site location, requested service, appointment date and time, and related notes.

Our appointment system may integrate with Google Calendar to manage operational scheduling. Information reasonably required to create and administer an appointment may be transmitted to the calendar service.

5. WhatsApp Communications

SmartBiz Fire may use WhatsApp and the WhatsApp Business Platform to communicate with customers and prospective customers.

Communications may include enquiries, appointment confirmations, inspection reminders, service updates, quotations, job communications, compliance reminders, renewal notifications and customer support.

By providing a WhatsApp number and requesting or agreeing to communications through WhatsApp, you acknowledge that information necessary to deliver those communications may be processed through the WhatsApp platform and its service providers.

You may request that SmartBiz Fire stop non-essential WhatsApp communications.

6. Sharing of Information

SmartBiz Fire does not sell personal information.

We may share limited personal information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide requested services. These may include providers supporting website hosting, business communications, WhatsApp messaging, calendar management, email delivery, accounting, data storage and security.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulatory requirements, legal proceedings, or legitimate security and fraud-prevention requirements.

7. Information Security

SmartBiz Fire takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, destruction or unlawful disclosure.

Measures may include access controls, authentication, restricted administrative access, secure infrastructure, monitoring, backups and other safeguards appropriate to our operations.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. SmartBiz Fire continually works to improve its security controls as its systems and services develop.

8. Information Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including operational, contractual, compliance, accounting, dispute-resolution, safety and legal requirements.

Information no longer reasonably required will be deleted, destroyed, de-identified or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request that SmartBiz Fire:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information about you
  • Provide access to applicable personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Update incomplete information
  • Delete or destroy information where legally appropriate
  • Stop or restrict certain processing where applicable
  • Address an objection or concern regarding processing

Some information may need to be retained where SmartBiz Fire has a legal, contractual, compliance, safety or legitimate operational obligation to retain it.

10. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies reasonably necessary for website functionality, security, performance, analytics and user experience. Where legally required, additional consent mechanisms may be provided for non-essential technologies.

11. Third-Party Services

Our website and operational systems may interact with third-party services. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Children's Privacy

SmartBiz Fire's commercial fire-safety services are not intended to collect personal information directly from children. If we become aware that children's personal information has been collected inappropriately through our digital services, we will take reasonable steps to address the matter.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, regulatory obligations or business processes change. The current version will be published on this page.
